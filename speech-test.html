Privacy and limitations

This test does not save or upload audio itself. Your browser's speech recognition may transmit audio to a browser/provider service. Microphone-test audio stays in your browser. Avoid testing with identifiable personal information.

Recognition of “sun” does not prove a child's /s/ articulation is correct. A grown-up should still assess clarity. On iPad, browser speech recognition may stop or require a fresh tap despite automatic retry logic.
